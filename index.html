Terms & Privacy

Last updated: 6 October 2026. By placing an order you agree to the terms below.

1. Our orders

Daily Parcel is a same-day order and the price shown is final. Order Booking is an advance order for parties and functions; the price shown is an estimate and the final quantity and price are confirmed with you by phone. An Enquiry is only a question and is not an order.

2. Payment (Cash on Delivery)

Payment is made to our delivery person when the order is delivered, for the full confirmed amount including any delivery charge. No online payment is taken on this website.

3. When your order is confirmed

Placing an order on the website is a request. It becomes a confirmed order when we confirm it with you by call or message. If we cannot reach you for the confirmation call, the order is treated as cancelled.

4. Cancellation and changes

Our food is freshly cooked for you and spoils quickly. You can cancel an order yourself from My Orders before we confirm it, and after confirmation as long as cooking has not started. Once cooking has started, or the order is out for delivery, the Cancel button stops working and the order cannot be cancelled or changed; please call us if you have a problem.

5. Delivery, refusal and unreachable customers

Our rider will wait up to 10 minutes at your address. If the order was confirmed and cooked as agreed, and you cannot be reached or you refuse to accept or pay for it, we may recover our reasonable actual loss (food, ingredients and delivery cost), up to the order value, including by sending a legal notice.

This does not apply if the food is wrong, spoiled, unsafe or not what you ordered. In that case tell the rider or call support immediately, with a photo if possible, and we will resolve it fairly.

6. Fake or prank orders

Orders must be placed with your real name, phone number and address. Placing an order with false details to cause loss to us may be an offence, including cheating under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, and we may report it to the police or cyber crime cell and take legal action.

7. Blocking

If orders are repeatedly refused without a valid reason, or are found to be fake, we may stop accepting orders from that phone number and address.

8. Food quality

If you have a problem with the food, call support on the number shown on this website as soon as you receive the order.

9. Your data and privacy

To process your order we collect: your name, phone number, email, delivery address, order details, date and time, and your notes. If delivery-area checking is on, we also collect your location when you tap the location button. We do not collect your location otherwise.

We use this data only to confirm, cook and deliver your order, show your orders in My Orders, prevent fake orders, and meet legal requirements. We do not sell your data. We may share it with our delivery staff, and with police, courts or authorities if the law requires. Our website, database and hosting providers (such as Google Firebase and Vercel) store data for us and may log technical details such as IP address as part of normal operation.

You can ask us to correct or delete your data by calling support. We may keep records we are legally required to keep, or that are needed for an unpaid or disputed order.

10. Law and courts

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Disputes will be handled by the courts having jurisdiction over the place of our kitchen. Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 are not affected.

11. Changes

We may update these terms. The terms shown when you place an order apply to that order.
